Flexure design uses the strut-and-tie method per EC2 Annex J. Tension tie force: Ftd = (NEd/2) × (pile CL − (φ/2 − 150)) / d, taking the critical section at 150mm inside the pile face (UK practice per CIRIA C108). Strut angle θ must be ≥ 21.8° per EC2 J.2.
Punching shear checked at the 2d control perimeter (EC2 §6.4.3). Beam shear at critical section with enhanced shear for short spans av/2d (EC2 §6.2.2(6)).
Design basis: BS EN 1992-1-1:2004 (EC2) + UK National Annex. Strut-and-tie model per Annex J. Pile forces by elastic rigid-cap analysis. Punching shear: §6.4.3. Beam shear: §6.2.2 with short-span enhancement. γc = 1.5, γs = 1.15.
